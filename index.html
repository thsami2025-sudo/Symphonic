          Storage & Data

          Audio you load plays straight from your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Inside this Claude preview, your audio files can't be kept in browser storage, so after a reload just re-add the same files/folder — favorites, playlists, and presets will automatically re-link to them by filename. Once you export this and run it as your own app or website, this changes automatically: your whole library (the actual audio, not just names) is saved on-device and restored every time you reopen it — no re-picking files.

          Packaging as an app: a plain "HTML-to-APK" WebView wrapper will play audio fine, but it usually won't show a lock-screen/notification media control — that needs either a Trusted Web Activity (which runs on real Chrome, so the notification appears automatically) or a proper app shell like Capacitor with its background-audio plugin. A generic WebView alone can't add this from JavaScript.
